# Your data stays with you

Measurements, settings, notes, invalid-shot marks, tags, and photos live in this browser. VelociTrack has no application backend, analytics, or hosted measurement database.

Caldwell sign-in goes directly from your browser to its Firebase service and reads your own account. It does not modify cloud measurements. Remembered sessions store tokens only after your opt-in; VelociTrack never saves passwords. Backups exclude sessions.

## What can remove local data?

- Browser/site cleanup or storage eviction.
- Removing a source and its data after confirmation.
- Replacing the workspace from a backup.

Marking a shot invalid is reversible and preserves its original reading. Removing a source and its data cannot be undone inside the app; it never deletes Caldwell cloud data. [Keep backups](backup.md) before large changes.

## What works offline?

The cached app and imported measurements work offline after successful initial setup.
The visual-guide text is cached with the app; walkthrough images and first-time
demo downloads need a connection unless already cached by the browser. Online
refresh needs a connection. Offline caching is not a backup.

The optional public demo is one owner-authorized example session with location and
private identifiers removed. It contains no credentials or general profile settings.
Adding it downloads a local copy; edits never go back to the host. Only the sample
and reviewed help illustrations are public, not ordinary users' imports or settings.

VelociTrack is an unofficial community project, independent of Caldwell and American Outdoor Brands. Provider access depends on service availability and rules.
